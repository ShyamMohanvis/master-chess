How to Play

Goal: Checkmate the opponent's king.

Move: Click a piece, then click a highlighted square to move.

Special moves: Castling, en passant, and pawn promotion are all supported.

Undo: Reverts your last move and the AI's response.

Timer: Each player has 10 minutes. If time runs out, that player loses.
